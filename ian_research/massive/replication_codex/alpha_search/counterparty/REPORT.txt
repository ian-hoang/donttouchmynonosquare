NAMED-COUNTERPARTY READTHROUGH: FEASIBILITY RESULT

Decision: do not price this candidate under the frozen rule. This screen did not
identify the minimum 10 source-validated events. No price or return data was fetched.
The rule was not broadened to manufacture a trade count.

What was screened
- All-issuer Massive 8-K disclosures dated 2024-01-01 through 2025-12-31.
- Fixed categories: deal termination (1,716 rows), deal breach/default (25),
  cybersecurity incident (114), natural-disaster impact (42), facility closure (155).
- Total: 2,052 disclosure rows, 1,945 unique filing accessions.
- The affected company had to be in the existing literal 99-ticker universe.
- A broad name screen found 183 potential third-party mentions. Another 8 rows
  mentioned only their own matched issuer, and 1,861 lacked a matched alias.

Result
Zero qualifying events were source-validated. The closest lead, Dell's January 30,
2024 filing about Broadcom/VMware, establishes termination of a commercial distribution
framework. It does not explicitly establish cancellation of committed purchases,
which the frozen rule requires. It remains an unvalidated near miss, not an eligible
short signal. The January 25 event date is distinct from its January 30 filing date.

The other operational near miss, Krispy Kreme ending its McDonald's relationship on
June 24, 2025, was checked against the 8-K and its contemporaneous joint press release.
The exhibit describes the product as a small, non-material part of McDonald's
breakfast business. Treating this as demonstrated material damage to McDonald's
would overstate the source. The frozen negative-customer rule does not qualify it.

Most mention hits were irrelevant: 135 described financing/equity arrangements and
21 described custody/administration replacements. Other hits were license options
declined by the large-cap company, contractual expirations, settlement liabilities,
unrelated company names, or statements without the required negative economic role.
All 183 candidates have recorded reasons in candidate_audit.json. Excerpt-based
exclusions are distinguished from the two filings and one exhibit actually reviewed.

Specific limitation
This was a minimum feasibility screen of the vendor's supporting excerpts, not an
exhaustive reading of 1,945 full filings and their exhibits. Parent/subsidiary aliases
are incomplete. Zero validated events does not mean no such events exist. It means
the readily available data and strict rule do not yet produce a backtestable cohort.
Full filings, historical link data and a wider tradable universe would be additional
work and a distinct study; they were not silently substituted here.
