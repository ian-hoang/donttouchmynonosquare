BEST READY-TO-USE INPUTS

1) SPY daily OHLCV, long history
Path: data/cache/world_cup/aggs_SPY.json
Format: JSON list, 5,470 entries, 2005-01-03 through 2026-09-30.
Fields: o,h,l,c,v,vw,t,n; t is epoch milliseconds at local market-date midnight (05:00 or 04:00 UTC).
Source provenance: alpha_ideas/world_cup/fetch.py fetched Massive /v2/aggs/ticker/SPY/range/1/day/2005-01-01/2026-09-30, adjusted=true.
Dividends: data/cache/world_cup/dividends_SPY.json, 94 records, ex_dividend_date and cash_amount; declaration_date exists for some records only. Dividends must be added to held-share returns when appropriate; OHLC adjusted=true is split-adjusted rather than dividend total-return data.
Splits: data/cache/world_cup/splits_SPY.json is an empty list (SPY no historical split records in this cached payload).
OHLC are aggregate trade prices, not simultaneous executable bid/ask quotes. Opening/closing strategy fills and slippage remain modeling assumptions.

2) ES hourly futures, long history
17 local yearly files under data/cache/GLBX.MDP3_ohlcv-1h_*.dbn.zst with DBN metadata symbols exactly ['ES.v.0','ES.v.1'].
Request: dataset GLBX.MDP3, symbols ['ES.v.0','ES.v.1'], schema ohlcv-1h, start 2010-06-07, end 2026-10-01, stype_in continuous; split at calendar-year boundaries.
Actual data: 189,516 rows, timestamps 2010-06-07 00:00 UTC to 2026-09-30 23:00 UTC.
Front volume rank: 97,256 hourly rows, 67 instrument IDs; second rank: 92,260 rows, 68 instrument IDs.
No duplicate timestamps within each symbol and no OHLC NaNs among present rows. Missing bars/holiday coverage are not ruled out.
Fields: rtype,publisher_id,instrument_id,open,high,low,close,volume,symbol. Index ts_event.
Cache-only loader: enumerate *.dbn.zst, DBNStore.from_file, inspect metadata.symbols before to_df. Existing loader/provenance: alpha_ideas/t1_shift/run.py, ES_REQ and _fully_cached. Import gqh.data if using its cache-key generator; do not permit loader cache-miss path to buy additional data.
Critical: ts_event is bar START. A 15:00 hourly bar closes at 16:00. An hourly 09:00 bar cannot supply a 09:30 open. gqh.data.stamp_bar_end() handles end timestamps. Use America/New_York local times with DST conversion and proper exchange holiday/early-close handling.
Roll handling: gqh.data.roll_safe_returns() compares same instrument_id at prior timestamp, using second rank for overlap. Do not calculate pct_change on a concatenated front-contract price series across rolls. A close-to-open holding must likewise use the same outright contract across both endpoints or explicitly model the roll trade.
All top-level GLBX minute caches were inspected for ES symbols; no long ES minute history was identified. ESU6 raw microstructure data exist under data/cache/micro_opening_2026 for August 2026; this is too short for a long-history independent study.

Columns Mkt-RF, SMB, HML, RF, quoted in percentage points. Loader gqh.analysis._french_csv('ff3') divides by 100 and reads cache without a network request. gqh.analysis.french_factors() additionally joins cached momentum.

Futures excess returns are not to have the full risk-free rate subtracted again as though they were funded SPY total returns; cash collateral and funding convention must be consistent.
