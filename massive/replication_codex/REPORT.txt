REPLICATION REPORT: CALL OVERLAY AFTER A DEAL-SIGNING 8-K

The frozen calculation replicates. No material event-trade discrepancies were found.

In-sample: all 40 keep/drop decisions match. All 30 valid trades match the saved
contracts, expiry, strikes, entry/exit dates, entry/exit parity spots, quotes and
costs. Maximum absolute net-return difference is 6.94e-18 (floating-point rounding).
2026: all 19 keep/drop decisions and all 15 published individual returns match.
The exact mean differs by 8.67e-19. Original individual 2026 returns were saved
only to 0.01 percentage-point precision; full original contract/price diagnostics
were not saved, so those details cannot be compared without rerunning old code.
Initial short-expiry spot estimates were also absent from the original cache.

One input discrepancy: the specification labels its universe as 100 tickers but
lists 99. We used the literal 99 throughout. Post-result inspection confirmed that
UNH is the only additional ticker in the original 100-name universe. The event
counts still match. Peer gaps differ from the original individual draws because
our peer pool excludes UNH and our fixed randomization convention need not
reproduce the original draws. No rule was adjusted to force a match.

METHOD AND LIMITS
Implementation and review followed the supplied frozen specification. The
coordinator had prior project context; pricing and audit agents started with the
specification and without that context. Own event and all 20-seed results were
finished and hash-recorded before comparison against the existing source and
outputs. The existing trading harness was never imported or executed.

No parameter tuning was performed. This repeats the already-tested 2026 rule;
it does not create another independent holdout test. Calendar completion cutoff
was fixed at 2026-10-02. All replication code and outputs are in this folder.
Raw API responses are only in cache/raw/, covered by this folder's .gitignore.

Conventions fixed before pricing: sample standard deviation (ddof=1), seeds
0 through 19 using Python random.Random, sorted eligible tickers and filing
dates, and a shared basket for same-date events. Each draw samples six tickers
before pricing. Invalid peers are excluded from that basket's average without
replacement. Cooldown and random draws walk continuously from 2024 into 2026.
Details and deterministic tie-breaking are recorded in conventions.json.

The specification permits daily marks up to three sessions old and same-day
entry quotes without a stricter intraday freshness requirement. Costs charge
twice the entry half-spread; there is no observed exit spread or commission.
Thus these are modeled marks and costs, not verified executable fills.
The required exit parity spot filter is retained even though it does not enter
overlay P&L. The peer rule uses the full +/-5 calendar-day disclosure window;
this includes future disclosures and is a retrospective comparison, not a
peer portfolio that could be selected with only information available at entry.
The positive peer gap alone does not establish causation or control for sector,
delta, volatility and other differences between event companies and peers.
With 30 and 15 event trades, replication establishes reproducibility of this
calculation; it does not by itself establish reliable executable alpha.
