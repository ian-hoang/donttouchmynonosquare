FIXED DEVELOPMENT EXPERIMENT: ACQUISITION CALL OVERLAY, SECTOR CONTROLS

Written before fetching this experiment's controls or computing their returns.
This is research on reused 2024-August 2026 dates, not a new holdout test.
We know the existing acquisition short-call signal before defining this test.
No parameter grid or best-seed selection is permitted.

Purpose: determine whether the existing lead survives quote execution and
comparison with sector peers that could have been selected at the time.

Event cohort: all 59 acquisition/merger company-days retained by the original
continuous >60-calendar-day cooldown, including the 14 previously dropped.
No new event exclusions based on exit price availability or old net return.
Use the literal 99-name universe from the replication spec.

Contract rule: preserve the frozen t_pre chain, initial spot estimator, 90-180
day expiry closest to 120 days, and first call strike >=105% of t_pre spot.
Entry is the session after the filing session; exit is ten sessions later.
Only selection metadata may be reused from saved replication observations.
Pre-event ATM and later stock daily-bar availability are not requirements here.

Fresh quotes: latest quote no later than five minutes before the actual session
close (15:55 New York, or 12:55 on early closes). Require quote age <=300 seconds,
finite bid/ask, 0<=bid<=ask, ask>0 and both displayed sizes >=1. Do not search
back after the most recent quote fails. No daily trade marks used for P&L.
Entry stock proxy: target-expiry ATM put-call parity using fresh entry quote
midpoints, r=.04, no dividend adjustment. Require positive stock proxy and
all entry quotes within 300 seconds of one another. This remains a proxy,
not observed stock execution or exact American-option parity.

Select controls before pricing: same broad economic sector as the event name,
excluding that issuer and any peer with any-tag disclosure dated within the
prior five calendar days plus the event filing date (day-5 through day). Future filings
are NEVER used to determine quiet eligibility. Draw up to three peers using
Python random.Random(1729) from sorted eligible names. Walk events in ascending
(filing_date,ticker) order; each event gets its own draw. No replacements.
The static sector mapping is fixed in prospective.py before fetching controls.

Use only sampled peers that have valid entry quotes, a decision observable at
entry. Once admitted at entry, a peer with missing exit remains an unresolved
basket leg: the basket is unpriced rather than silently dropping that loser.
Event exit failure is also recorded explicitly. Full eligibility and attrition
counts are reported; no win-conditional filtering is allowed.

Fees: $0.65 per option contract per side, or $0.013 per share round trip.
Short call net = (entry bid - exit ask - .013) / entry stock proxy.
Long call net = (exit bid - entry ask - .013) / entry stock proxy.

Primary strategy remains the covered-call overlay on already-held shares.
Primary research comparison: event short-call net minus equal-weight mean
short-call net of its same-sector controls. This is a comparison of strategies,
not P&L of a tradable long/short portfolio.
Secondary trade-expression diagnostic: event short-call net plus equal-weight
long-call net of those controls. Report per dollar of event stock reference
notional AND divided by two for the sum of event and peer reference notionals.
This is a different options position, not one of the challenge's five standard
strategies, not delta/vega neutral, not a capital or margin return, and not a
riskless hedge. Fractional peer sizing is a notional diagnostic.

Report every event and selected peer; mean, median, SD, win rate, unannualized
per-trade mean/SD; simple date-cluster and month-block bootstrap intervals;
leave-one-issuer-out ranges. Intervals are descriptive after the existing
large strategy search and do not correct its multiple testing.

Keep code/output only in this alpha_search directory. Keep newly cached raw
responses in its ignored cache. Never edit the original frozen result files.
