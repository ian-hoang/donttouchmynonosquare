FROZEN FEASIBILITY SCREEN: NEGATIVE NAMED-COUNTERPARTY READTHROUGH
Frozen 2026-10-03, before this screen fetches candidate filings and before any returns.

Economic hypothesis
An issuer's public operational disclosure can contain adverse information about another
company that investors following only the latter company's own news overlook. The
direction is negative for a named supplier losing committed orders, or a named customer
explicitly suffering interruption of contracted supply. This is a directional, long-put
hypothesis, not a generic short-volatility return claim.

Source supporting the mechanism, not this exact strategy:
Cohen and Frazzini (2008), Economic Links and Predictable Returns, Journal of Finance.
https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~afrazzin/pdf/Economic%20Links%20and%20Predictable%20Returns%20-%20Cohen%20and%20Frazzini.pdf
The paper studies delayed incorporation of news across publicly known customer-supplier
links. Its historical stock results do not validate current large-cap option profits.

Data / universe
Signal issuers: all issuers covered by Massive's 8-K disclosure endpoint.
Dates: filing_date 2024-01-01 through 2025-12-31 only. No 2026 outcomes.
Tradable counterparties: the literal 99-ticker universe in replication_codex/events.py.
Candidate categories fixed now: deal_termination, deal_breach_default,
cybersecurity_incident, natural_disaster_impact, facility_closure.
These categories are confirmed against massive/research/taxonomy.csv before fetching.

Eligibility, all required
1. The disclosing issuer and affected counterparty are distinct economic companies.
2. The contemporaneous disclosure names the affected counterparty unambiguously.
3. One of exactly two negative actions is explicitly documented:
   (a) the disclosing customer terminates or suspends committed purchases from the
       named large-cap supplier; or
   (b) the disclosing supplier cannot perform contracted delivery, and the document
       explicitly identifies an adverse supply/operational consequence for its named
       large-cap customer.
4. There is a real commercial supply/customer link; co-mentions, financing, law firms,
   generic market commentary, litigation between firms, and M&A counterparties do not
   qualify. Merger, acquisition, spin-off and financing agreement terminations excluded.
5. Role and adverse action must be supported by the actual filing/exhibit, not an AI
   category alone. Preserve the filing accession, original URL, filing date, quoted
   evidence, affected ticker, role, and exclusion reason for rejected candidates.
6. No hypothetical future risk, contemplated renegotiation, mere expiration, or voluntary
   alternate sourcing qualifies. Repeated updates of the same incident are one event;
   keep the first documented qualifying filing per counterparty/incident, with a
   60-calendar-day receiver-level cooldown applied before pricing.

Feasibility decision
At least 10 source-validated events are required to advance to exploratory option pricing.
Below 10, record the specific coverage/sparsity limitation; do not relax this rule.
No option or underlying return is fetched or inspected by this screen.

Frozen prospective trade, only if feasible
Buy the nearest-ATM put with expiry 90-180 calendar days away, nearest 120 days,
at the close of the first trading session strictly after the qualifying filing date.
Exit after 10 trading sessions. Use contemporaneous executable ask at entry and bid
at exit plus $0.65 per contract per side; maximum quote age 300 seconds and positive
bid/ask sizes. No stale daily trade-close substitution. Choose the contract only with
information available at entry. Report a matched same-day, same-sector and pre-event
volatility comparison; benchmark absence is an explicit limitation, not zero return.
Both absolute net put P&L and incremental performance must be reported. Contract
selection/spot availability and earnings overlap need a separate frozen implementation
before pricing. This feasibility screen makes no alpha claim.

Hazards
An 8-K may repeat earlier public news; source timing must be recorded. AI tagging
availability is retrospective. Static-universe survival, missing smaller suppliers,
uncertain materiality to a large counterparty, and selection on named relationships
limit generalization. The direction cannot be inferred from a name alone. Existing
2026 strategy research has already contaminated that year as a general holdout.
