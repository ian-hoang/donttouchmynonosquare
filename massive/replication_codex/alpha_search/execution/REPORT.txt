LIMITS
This tests execution on the old valid-trade cohort, excluding the original 14 event
pricing failures. The original peer filter used future filings within five days, and
strict-invalid peer exits are removed here. These comparisons are retrospective diagnostics.
They must not be marketed as a prospectively executable peer-selection strategy.
The root prospective experiment separately addresses peer selection and attrition.
Static universe, retrospective tags, original parity denominators, no delta/vega matching,
no assignment/dividends/funding or market-impact model remain. All 2026 data are reused.
Positive means do not establish independent statistically validated alpha.
