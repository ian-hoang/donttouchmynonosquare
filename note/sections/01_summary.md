## 1. Summary
We turn public CEO interviews into a daily equity signal. For {funnel.dated_in_tenure_window|int} curated YouTube interviews of 20 mega-cap CEOs (2016–2026) we extract facial action proxies (MediaPipe face mesh, 52 blendshapes), voice quality (openSMILE eGeMAPS) and word use (Loughran–McDonald, Larcker–Zakolyukina categories) from the CEO's own segments only, score each against that CEO's last 30 interviews, and combine the cues with **signs and weights fixed in advance from meta-analytic effect sizes**. We short high-stress interviews and buy calm ones, beta-hedged with SPY, for 20 sessions. In-sample (2016–Sep 2024, {primary_IS.n_traded|int} trades) the strategy's net Sharpe is **{primary_IS.sharpe}** (costs ×2: {costs_x2_IS.sharpe}); on the sealed out-of-sample period (Oct 2024–Sep 2026, evaluated once) it is **{oos.sharpe}**. **Neither number survives scrutiny.** In-sample the strategy fails our own pre-registered kill condition (FF5+UMD alpha t = {primary_IS.ff_alpha_t}); out-of-sample the signal has the wrong sign (rank IC {oos.event_tests.ic_ic|f3}). Two candidates that looked promising in-sample (voice-only, QQQ hedge; both ≈0.2) also turn negative once the sealed window is opened. Our conclusion is a well-powered null: across {funnel.processed|int} interviews and {primary_IS.n_traded|int} + {oos.n_traded|int} trades, the stress visible in mega-cap CEOs' faces and voices is already in the price at a 20-day horizon. Every follow-up we registered after the failure also fails: 192-variant optimization (winner −0.87 out-of-sample), walk-forward selection (−0.54), the same signal on 20 less-watched CEOs, and post-interview drift. Since October 2024 the score's sign has reversed in both universes (pooled IC −0.12), which we flag as the next hypothesis to test rather than trade.
