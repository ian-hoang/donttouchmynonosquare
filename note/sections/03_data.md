## 3. Data and universe
**Universe.** 20 CEOs of US large caps chosen by role and video availability, never by returns, inside their verified CEO tenures and after listing (Cook ends 2026-08-31, Iger 2026-03-17, Gelsinger 2024-12-01, Saylor 2022-08-08; all date corrections are logged in `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`). **Corpus.** Two rounds of YouTube search ({funnel.search_hits_round1|int} + {funnel.search_hits_round2|int} hits; round 2 added year-specific queries because round 1 skewed to 2023–26) → {funnel.rule_prefiltered|int} after rule filters → {funnel.agent_curated_keep|int} kept by LLM curation agents that saw **metadata only, never prices** (unscripted Q&A only; keynotes, compilations, re-uploads, AI fakes and "about the CEO" videos removed) → {funnel.dated_in_tenure_window|int} dated inside tenure windows → {funnel.processed|int} processed → {funnel.identity_qc_pass|int} with the CEO's face verified. **Event time** is the YouTube upload timestamp (UTC, to the second); a re-upload can only make an event later. **Prices**: Yahoo Finance daily OHLCV, split- and dividend-adjusted (we trade open-to-open; a returns fingerprint is committed). **Factors**: Ken French FF5 + momentum. **Earnings dates**: SEC EDGAR 8-K Item 2.02 filings. Interviews within ±2 sessions of an earnings filing are excluded ({events.excluded_near_earnings|int} events). **Survivorship**: Intel (Gelsinger) and MicroStrategy (Saylor) are kept for their tenures; the universe is today's well-known CEOs, which biases toward names that did well, so every trade is hedged and the signal is long–short *within* each CEO's own history (name-level drift cancels).
