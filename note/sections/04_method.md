## 4. Methodology
**Measurement (per video, 12-minute window).** On UF HiPerGator: MediaPipe face mesh (52 blendshapes, head pose) and pose at 4 fps; Whisper large-v3-turbo on GPUs. ArcFace identifies the CEO as **the face that recurs across that CEO's interviews**; an audio-visual vote over ≤15 s utterances assigns speech to the CEO. **Cues** (signs, weights ∝ meta-analytic |d|; `config/signal_spec.json`): face: chin raise (+0.25), lip press (+0.16), facial pleasantness (−0.12), illustrator gestures (−0.085); voice: pitch (+0.195), jitter/shimmer/HNR as vocal-tension proxies; text: negations, third-person plural, general-knowledge references, extreme positive emotion (+), anxiety, numbers, shareholder value (−); plus a verbal–nonverbal incongruence term (upbeat words × tense face). Cues that do *not* replicate in meta-analyses (gaze aversion, blinking, self-touch, head motion, smiling, pauses, fillers, speech rate) are excluded and form the FOLK placebo. **Normalization.** Each cue is a robust z-score against the same CEO's ≤30 strictly earlier interviews (≥5 required). TELL is the equal-weight mean of the face, voice, text and incongruence scores (≥5 cues across ≥2 modalities). **Signal and trade.** S = −TELL / σ(TELL of all earlier events). Decision time is upload + 2 h; we enter at the next regular-session open and exit 20 sessions later (open-to-open); a new interview replaces that CEO's open position. Size: a full-signal position targets 10% annualized idiosyncratic volatility (≤35% notional per event, ≤50% per name, ≤200% gross), hedged with SPY at the 252-day ex-ante beta. **Costs**: 5 bps/side on stocks, 1 bp on SPY, 30 bps/yr borrow; costs ×2 always reported. **Inference**: Newey–West FF5+UMD alpha, bootstrap CIs, Deflated Sharpe over {dsr.n_trials|int} logged trials, permutation and random-date nulls. **No lookahead, enforced:** a test suite rewrites all data after t and asserts that no signal, weight or return before t changes; `run_all.py` refuses to run if it fails.
