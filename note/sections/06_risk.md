## 6. Risk management
**Limits (pre-set, in `config/strategy.json`).** Each event is sized to 10% annualized idiosyncratic volatility at full signal, capped at 35% notional; any one name at 50%; gross at 200% including the hedge. A new interview replaces the CEO's existing position, so exposure never stacks on one name. **Market and factor risk.** Every leg is hedged with SPY at its 252-day ex-ante beta. On FF5+UMD the strategy's market beta is {primary_IS.ff_beta_Mkt-RF|f2}, momentum beta {primary_IS.ff_beta_Mom|f2}, and R² {primary_IS.ff_r2|f2}: returns are not a disguised factor. **Tail and regime.** Daily 99% VaR {extra.var_99|pct2}, CVaR {extra.cvar_99|pct2}, worst day {extra.worst_day|pct2}; {extra.pct_months_positive|pct} of months positive; Calmar {extra.calmar}. Stress windows (COVID crash, 2022 bear market, SVB, Aug-2024 vol spike) are in the appendix. **De-risking rule.** A drawdown brake (halve exposure while drawdown exceeds 10%) is pre-declared and reported as a variant; it is off in the headline so the headline is the simplest rule. **Concentration.** Results by CEO and leave-one-CEO-out runs (appendix) check that no single CEO drives the result.
