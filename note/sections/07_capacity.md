## 7. Liquidity and capacity
The universe is the most liquid US equities (median ADV of names traded ≈ {capacity.median_adv_usd|musd}). At $10M AUM a typical trade is {capacity.participation_at_10m|pct2} of ADV. We charge 5 bps per side; the Abdi–Ranaldo (2017) close-high-low estimator puts the median effective half-spread of these names at a few bps (appendix), so 5 bps also covers slippage at the open. Turnover is low ({primary_IS.turnover_x_per_year|f2}× capital per year) because positions last 20 sessions. With square-root impact (cost = fixed + 0.7·σ·√(Q/ADV)), the net Sharpe falls to half the gross Sharpe at about **{capacity.aum_net_sharpe_half_gross|musd}** of AUM. Capacity is limited by the number of interviews, not by liquidity: the strategy holds about one or two names at a time, so it is a sleeve within a larger book, not a standalone fund.
