## 8. Limitations and next steps
Upload time is a conservative proxy for broadcast time; TV airtimes (GDELT) would move entries earlier. Identity and speaker attribution are automated and imperfect (CEO speech share is ~40% of window words by design, to avoid attributing interviewer speech). Twenty CEOs and roughly one event per CEO per month limit statistical power; the Deflated Sharpe and minimum-track-record length quantify this. The universe is defined by availability of interviews, which favors famous, successful companies; hedging and within-CEO normalization reduce but do not remove this. **Next steps:** speaker diarization (ElevenLabs Scribe) for response latency; airtime-based entries with minute bars; earnings-call video for many more firms; the TELL score as a volatility input for options.
