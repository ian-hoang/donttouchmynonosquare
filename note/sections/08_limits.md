## 8. Why it failed, limitations, and next steps
**Candidate explanations.** (1) *Efficiency where attention is highest*: these are the 20 most-watched CEOs in the world, and every interview is dissected by analysts and retail investors, which is exactly where underreaction to soft information should be smallest. (2) *Wrong outcome or horizon*: the closest prior work (Koo et al. 2026) finds that interview incongruence predicts accounting outcomes (negative earnings surprises, restatements) rather than 20-day returns; the price effect may arrive only at the next earnings release, mixed with everything else. (3) *Timing*: YouTube upload time can trail broadcast by hours, so some reaction happens before our entry. (4) *Measurement noise*: 12-minute windows at 360p and automated identity and speaker attribution. **Limitations.** Survivorship in the universe (mitigated by hedging and within-CEO normalization); 14 videos lost their audio. **Next steps.** Test the same pipeline on (a) accounting outcomes and the next earnings surprise, (b) earnings-call video for the Russell 1000, where attention is lower, (c) broadcast airtimes with minute bars, and (d) the unconditional post-interview drift as its own pre-registered hypothesis.
