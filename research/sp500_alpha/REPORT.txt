OUTCOME
No candidate establishes a reliable live alpha or Sharpe2. The strongest recent lead
is the fixed SPY pullback-in-uptrend rule: recent net excess Sharpe0.99, falling only
to0.89 when assumed execution costs are tripled. It nevertheless failed the2022-2023
validation period. Retain it as a research candidate; do not label it validated.
Pre-FOMC overnight is the other positive lead but has only22recent trades and weak
incremental evidence after controlling ordinary calendar effects.

These are actual historical return tests, not data-readiness exercises. They use ETF
daily histories and about2.37million SPY minute bars. No unavailable stock-loan dataset
was assumed, no simulated prices were used for performance, and no paid data purchase
was made. Synthetic tests were used only to verify accounting/timing.

THE PULLBACK RULE, FIXED BEFORE ITS RETURNS
1. At close, SPY is above its200-session moving average.
2. Its three-session total return is below -1.5times the standard deviation of daily
   returns over the previous20sessions, excluding today's return from volatility.
3. Buy SPY at the following session's open, fully funded, with no short or leverage.
4. Keep the number of shares fixed. Exit at the following open after either a close
   at/above the five-session average or the fifth held session, whichever comes first.
5. Keep idle capital in the assumed cash benchmark. Charge1bp per side, stress3bp.

WHAT WAS VERIFIED
Independent ledger and dividend-entitlement checks, five-session holding and next-open
execution tests, five prefix-causality replays, and peer code review for the pullback.
An independent price-feed sensitivity on identical2024-September30,2026dates gives
Yahoo/Massive excessSharpes0.95615/0.95625, with all42trade timings matching. Both
retain negative validation. This supports data consistency, not alpha significance.
Other studies include contract/signal checks, full bidirectional trading costs,
chronological splits and declared baselines. Source-definition correction to the
published breakout also fails; it remains separately reported and is not a new alpha.
Intraday eligibility was corrected to avoid excluding sessions using future full-day
bar completeness; original results remain preserved and failures remain failures.

Historical auction/opening prices plus slippage are execution assumptions, not NBBO-
confirmed fills or institutional capacity. Finalized minute aggregates can include
late revisions unavailable at signal time. The slower rule uses previous-day data
and executes the next morning, reducing but not proving away vendor-vintage concerns.
Dividend cash is recognized ex-date, an approximation to actual payment-date funding.

SELECTION DISCIPLINE
Each family's rule was fixed before its own outcomes. The breakout was added after
earlier sector/calendar results; the pullback was added after seven earlier candidates.
All eight and the source-definition/accounting diagnostics are disclosed. This
chronological holdout is not globally untouched: prior research and published source
selection already used overlapping history. The batch-level multiplicity adjustment
does not correct the full history of ideas and author choices.

PRACTICAL RESEARCH DECISION
Prioritize the unchanged SPY pullback rule for an independently recorded forward
paper test and execution validation, alongside an explicit SPY/cash benchmark. Keep
pre-FOMC overnight as a secondary small-sample lead. Do not tune new lookbacks or
thresholds on the already-inspected2024-2026period to force Sharpe2.
