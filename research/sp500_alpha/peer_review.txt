Noise-band/VWAP implementation:
The trailing 14-session mean is shifted one session, so current-day returns do not enter their own noise estimate. Cumulative VWAP uses only preceding/current completed minutes. Its units are sum(price times volume)/sum(volume); neither share volume nor turnover is mislabeled as an execution quote.
Completed minute index 29 corresponds to 09:59–10:00; entry at index 31 is the 10:01 opening-price proxy. The same rule applies at each half-hour through 15:30. Share count is retained while direction is unchanged. Reversal closes the old position before sizing the new position. Fees are charged to both legs; entry sizing reserves the opening fee and caps entry exposure. Borrow is charged on maximum observed opening-mark short notional during the day; funding charges absolute notional-minutes using lagged DGS3MO. The cash ledger equals independently reconstructed holding P&L minus fees.
Data-quality decisions use the prefix observed by each half-hour. A subsequently missing exit cannot erase an earlier entry; it makes the outcome unresolved. Delayed fills use the first later observed opening price. The final 15:59 opening exit is a trade-print assumption, not NBBO execution proof. Causal entry checks pass; real-time vendor data availability remains a separate issue.

Material data observation and interpretation:
January 2026 sample has 7,800 RTH minute bars, all positive volume. 274 vendor minute VWAP values lie outside that minute’s OHLC range. Largest sampled gap: 2026-01-20 15:41 ET, high 677.22, low 677.03, vendor VWAP 684.8542 (112.73 bp above high). This is not automatically data corruption: the vendor documents different trade-condition eligibility for price and volume. Average-price and odd-lot trades can update volume without updating OHLC. Do not silently clamp, delete or retune on these observations.
The vendor also documents minute-bar recalculation for late trades and end-of-day inclusion of trades arriving outside its update buffer. Consequently, finalized REST minute VWAP and OHLC may differ from their live signal-time values. A one-minute processing delay does not by itself eliminate this issue. As-received tape reconstruction or prospective shadow capture would be needed to establish availability and executable performance. No raw-trade replay or live fill/capacity audit was performed here.
