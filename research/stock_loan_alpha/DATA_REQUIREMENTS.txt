Minimum historical input package

1. Stock-loan observations and allocations
   Stable security ID; broker/provider; anonymized account/contract ID; observation
   and fund-received UTC timestamps; quote expiry; firm/indicative flag; executable
   quantity; annual fee OR net rebate with explicit convention; day-count basis;
   collateral rules; proceeds-interest rules; usable settlement time; loan allocation
   timestamp, quantity and ID; term, rerating and recall rights. Include failed locates.

2. Realized loan and account cashflows
   Dated settled quantity, collateral base, fee and credit rates/charges, loan returns,
   recalls/buy-ins, debit funding, margin, substitute dividends and actual fees.
   Preserve weekends/holidays and intraday rate changes. A current fee snapshot does
   not replace the realized path. Net rebates must not also be charged a gross fee.

3. Independent stock and option observations
   Stock NBBO and full option call/put NBBO updates, received/SIP timestamps, sizes,
   quote conditions, cancellations, halt states, exact contract IDs/strike/expiry,
   multiplier/exercise style/deliverable. Execution/complex-order records if available.
   Do not infer stock price from the put-call relation that is being tested.

4. Event/lifecycle observations
   Voting record date and when publicly known; where available voting recall/re-lend
   records; dividends and announcements as known then; corporate actions/delistings;
   exercise/assignment records or a separately declared conservative scenario model.

Provider name or a local export path is sufficient to start an adapter audit.
No credentials are required in chat. No provider purchase or live trade is authorized
or performed by this research package.

Indicative historical fees may support a separately labelled association study.
Short interest, short volume, utilization, EOD option mids, and proxy-calendar dates
do not replace executed or firm lending terms for a tradability/performance claim.
