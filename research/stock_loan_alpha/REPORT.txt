STOCK-LOAN SUPPLY RESTORATION VS OPTION SCARCITY
Assessment date: 2026-10-03

RESULT
Economically coherent research hypothesis; no historical alpha test is possible with
the lending observations currently located. No market strategy P&L or Sharpe was
computed. This is a data-availability and economics result, not rejection of the idea.

The proposed position is a reversal: buy one call at ask, sell the matching put at
bid, short 100 shares at bid. Its possible edge is lagged repricing after an observed
improvement in borrowing, not the payoff identity itself.

MOST IMPORTANT ECONOMIC DISTINCTION
Cheap overnight borrow and expensive 30-60 day option-implied borrowing can coexist
without mispricing. Options may reflect expected renewed scarcity, lender recall risk,
funding differences or early exercise. Securing shares does not necessarily secure the
rate or the duration. Compare matched borrowing horizons and actual contractual rights;
otherwise test a risky forecast of future borrowing costs, not locked arbitrage.

The cited BlackRock letter supports the recall mechanism but expressly challenges
automatic relending after the record date. It contains no evidence that options lag
restored lending supply. The record date, public disclosure time and loan restoration
time are three different clocks. See economics.txt and sources.json.

AVAILABLE-DATA AUDIT
The read-only inventory examined 190,077 cached JSON payloads (3.58 GB), classifying
the first result's fields and checking first/last observation dates. It found 51,909
NBBO payloads and 17,437 option-contract payloads, but no recognized stock-loan fee,
rebate, lendable supply, locate or recall fields. Broader filename/code searches also
located no historical lending dataset. This does not prove such information cannot
exist under another name or in a separate provider account.

Cached quote dates range from January 2024 to August 2026; they are sparse requests,
not a complete synchronized stock/options panel. Three sample GM call-put pairs have
observations on both dates of earlier unrelated research. One sample's entry call/put
timestamps differ by 24.65 seconds. This fails the proposed conservative synchronization
screen; timestamps alone do not prove an older standing quote was no longer actionable.
None of those sample pairs is established as a voting or supply-restoration event.

Massive's documented short-interest and short-volume feeds do not provide loan prices
or lending commitments. Indicative historical lending rates may support an association
study but not proof of account-specific executable borrow. See data_audit/REPORT.txt
for primary provider documentation and data_audit/cache_inventory.json for scope.

Synthetic fixtures verify accounting, not economic profitability. With unchanged
prices and a $0.02 stock spread plus $0.10 spreads in each option, one 100-share package
loses $22 crossing all three legs twice. This is an invented example, not a trade.
On $102 collateral per share, one additional annual percentage point of borrow cost
over 30 days costs about $0.084 per share under ACT/365; this is arithmetic only.

The helper is not a complete backtester. It does not implement loan-event detection,
an American-option valuation engine, account-specific funding forecasts, lifecycle
simulation or portfolio sizing. It refuses a normal three-leg close after a reported
lifecycle event or when that event history is unknown.

PROPOSED EMPIRICAL TEST
Define restoration independently of options: same-provider firm borrow fee falls by
at least half from a prior five-session median of at least 10%, executable quantity
increases, and a second observation confirms it. Use the first qualifying event within
ten business days after a record date known by entry. These are proposed fixed research
thresholds, not values fitted to outcomes. Calendar association is not proof of cause.

Select one near-ATM standard 30-60 day expiry before comparing strikes, secure the
borrow, and examine the executable three-leg residual with term-matched financing.
Keep floating and contractually committed lending cohorts separate. The proposed
primary hold is five trading sessions, with earlier lifecycle exits. A maturity hold
would be a separately reported strategy. Final entry valuation and uncertainty buffer
require actual account/loan terms and must be frozen before performance is inspected.

Compare against ordinary hard-to-borrow reversals, non-voting supply restorations and
post-record-date days without observed restoration. Include failed allocations, missing
quotes, recalls and assignment costs. Do not condition on loans that survived.
Calculate daily liquidation-marked returns on documented allocated capital; retain
idle days and overlapping exposure. A small or nearly zero net premium is not capital.

NEXT REQUIRED INPUT
Historical borrow quotes/allocations and realized loan terms, identified by provider
or local file path. Actual available quantities, rate convention, receive timestamps,
rerating/recall rights and subsequent charges matter. Once supplied, first audit the
coverage and joinability; then finalize the frozen pilot before examining returns.
Until then, this idea has no validated performance estimate and no evidence of Sharpe 2.
