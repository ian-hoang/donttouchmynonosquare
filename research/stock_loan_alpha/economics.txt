STOCK-LOAN SUPPLY RESTORATION / OPTION SCARCITY: ECONOMICS AUDIT
Research cutoff: 2026-10-03, America/New_York
Scope: execution and financing model; no market backtest, prices, or performance claims.

VERDICT
The proposed direction is correct: buy the call, sell the same-strike/same-expiry put,
and sell short the matching shares. This is a reversal. It benefits if the synthetic
long is sufficiently inexpensive relative to physical stock after all actual carry.
The unexplored prediction is a lag in executable option repricing after observed
borrow relief. A vote calendar alone does not establish this prediction.

The transaction is established market structure, not a newly invented arbitrage.
Any novelty must come from supply-restoration timing, access, forecasting, or execution.
Net Sharpe 2 cannot be inferred from the payoff diagram or narrow observed basis.

WHAT THE BLACKROCK LETTER DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
BlackRock's December 14, 2021 letter discusses the choice between lending income and
voting. Its relevant clock is the voting RECORD DATE, not the shareholder meeting.
The letter disputes the assumption that recalled shares can immediately be lent again:
borrowers may replace loans elsewhere, leaving a returning lender waiting for demand.
It also notes that record dates can precede definitive proxy disclosures. [S1]

This supports a conditional mechanism and important timing caution. It provides no
options evidence, average borrow-rate decline, persistence estimate, or alpha result.
Do not infer market-wide loan availability from one fund's subsequent willingness to
lend. Do not backfill record dates from later proxy documents into an earlier signal.
Restoration must be measured in executable availability and account-specific terms.

PAYOFF AND SIGN
For a standard contract, use +1 call, -1 put and -100 shares. Both contracts must
have identical strike K, expiration, exercise style, deliverable and multiplier.
Adjusted contracts require the actual OCC deliverable; exclude them in a first pilot.
The OIC explicitly identifies this package as a reverse conversion. [S2]

Define all quotes per underlying share. For matched unexercised positions closed at u:

Entry cash = S_bid(t) - C_ask(t) + P_bid(t).
Exit cash  = C_bid(u) - P_ask(u) - S_ask(u).
Trading P&L = entry cash + exit cash.

Net P&L = trading P&L + incremental account financing cash flows
          - stock-borrow charges - substitute dividends
          - commissions, exchange/regulatory fees and extra execution costs.

Multiply by actual matched quantity. This directly executable before-expiry ledger
does not require pricing American options as European options. Simultaneous-looking
quotes still do not establish simultaneous fills. Verify displayed size, quote age,
exchange eligibility and short-stock feasibility; apply realistic sequencing/latency.

For a hypothetical perfectly matched expiry delivery without early exercise or pin
problems, terminal option/stock cash is -K per share, so gross trading cash is
S_bid(t) - C_ask(t) + P_bid(t) - K. Interest and all costs remain separate. Treat this
as an identity check, not the default realized cash flow for American equity options.

SCARCITY INTERPRETATION
Under restrictive European, deterministic-rate, continuous-yield assumptions,
C_E - P_E = S exp(-(q + ell) tau) - K exp(-r tau), where ell is an effective stock
lending/convenience yield. Greater effective scarcity lowers C_E-P_E and makes the
synthetic long cheaper. Thus the proposed sign is consistent with expensive implied
scarcity and cheaper available physical borrow. This equation is a diagnostic only:
an effective implied yield is not an account's quoted borrow fee.

For American contracts C_A-P_A also includes the difference in early-exercise premia.
Dividends, asymmetric funding, specials, corporate actions and stale quotes can produce
an apparent implied-borrow discrepancy without an executable opportunity. Use a model
with discrete dividends and American exercise if reporting implied yields. Prefer
actual cash P&L for acceptance and rejection rather than one inferred yield number.

FINANCING: AVOID DOUBLE COUNTING
Maintain segregated incremental cash balances under the actual prime-broker agreement:
restricted short proceeds/collateral, option premium cash, additional posted margin,
and freely usable or debit cash. Collateral transfers themselves are not expenses.
Cash collateral earns only the contractual credit, not automatically the risk-free
rate. Financing additional required collateral is a separate funding cost when real.

IBKR's published convention separates a stock-borrow fee and interest on short-sale
proceeds, with costs based on settled positions. Its page also reports a net rebate
and warns that rates vary by arrangement. [S3] Implement one of two mutually exclusive
conventions:
  A. Separate fee convention: credit actual proceeds interest and subtract the stock
     borrow fee on its specified collateral base.
  B. Already-net rebate convention: book the contractual net stock-loan cash flow
     once, plus any genuinely separate funding or service fees.
Never both subtract a fee embedded in net rebate and subtract it again. Never credit
full proceeds at r and then add a rebate on the same money.

Every day use the specified collateral mark, rate base, day count, settled quantity
and accrual days, including relevant weekends/holidays. IBKR documentation describes
a 102% marked/rounded collateral convention; this can matter for low-priced stock.
The agreement, not a blanket multiplier, governs an institutional account. [S4]
Book dividend obligations when incurred and settle them on the applicable payment
date; reflect any actual tax gross-up or special corporate-action compensation.

PRE-BORROW IS NOT A TERM-RATE GUARANTEE
IBKR's pre-borrow documentation distinguishes indicative from actual rates and says
rates can change; its documented program computes standard rates nightly. [S4]
An actual locate is also not proof that a lender will maintain the loan to expiry.
The lending agreement must identify quantity, term, rerating and recall provisions.
The broker's returns/recalls guide explicitly allows recall. [S5]

If only floating overnight loans are available, projected profit must use scenarios
for future fees and availability. Ex post realized fees belong in outcome accounting,
not the entry decision. Include rejected locates, unfilled quantities and forced
closeouts. A backtest conditioned on loans that survived would select away failures.

AMERICAN EXERCISE AND CORPORATE ACTIONS
The short put may be assigned on any business day, and early exercise becomes relevant
for deep in-the-money puts. Exact assignment is not deterministic. [S6]
On put assignment: debit K times shares, receive shares against the short, return the
borrow as appropriate, and dispose of or hedge the remaining long call at executable
prices. Do not continue the old three-leg P&L afterward.
On voluntary call exercise: debit K times shares, receive shares against the short,
and close the remaining short put. If a buy-in covers the stock first, close both
option legs; the package otherwise becomes an unhedged synthetic long.

Exclude expiries and ex-dividend crossings in an initial narrow pilot, but this is not
proof of zero exercise risk. Lack of dividends does not by itself eliminate all
hard-to-borrow exercise considerations. Implement stressed adverse assignment timing,
notification lag, exercise fees and closing spreads, not just a model-optimal policy.
OIC notes that exercise need not be certain even for in-the-money expiry contracts,
and broker restrictions/halts complicate exercise. [S6, S7]

MINIMUM OBSERVATIONS FOR A CREDIBLE TEST
1. Timestamped stock and call/put bid/ask plus sizes, condition codes and contract IDs.
2. Timestamped firm borrow availability, account-specific rate and actual executed
   loan terms, quantity, rate changes, recalls/returns and broker charges.
3. Point-in-time known dividends/corporate actions; actual compensation outcomes.
4. Actual account cash, collateral, margin and credit/debit interest conventions.
5. Public-availability timestamps for voting record dates if used as predictors.
6. Assignment/exercise records or conservative scenario treatment for hypothetical
   portfolios; expired/delisted/adjusted securities cannot simply disappear.

Analyst aggregate borrow fees, indicative lists, short interest, utilization and
short-sale volume can help screen or contextualize. None establishes a secured loan
at the claimed execution price and size. EOD option mids cannot establish this edge.

FALSIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE
Freeze liquidity, maturity, event, exit and quote-age rules before outcomes. Test
observed loan relief against comparable non-voting loan relief and matched no-relief
days. Require that option repricing follows the genuinely available borrow quote
after an implementable latency. Include dividends, actual funding, two-sided spreads,
borrow rerating, failed fills and recalls. An edge that appears only in inferred
European parity, stale quotes, mid-prices, post-event data, or guaranteed-borrow
assumptions fails the executable hypothesis.

Report daily liquidation-marked P&L on fixed allocated capital, not profit divided by
near-zero net option premium. Show margin/stock-loan capacity and concentration.
Multiple strikes and maturities on one stock/date are not independent experiments.
Bootstrap by event/date clusters and retain zero-position days. Inspect recall and
assignment tails; a smooth held-to-expiry accrual series can overstate Sharpe.
